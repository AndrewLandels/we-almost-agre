“You should invest in Bitcoin” is a complete life instruction wearing the clothes of a market take. That is why it starts fights.

The seed map is [here](/claim/bitcoin). Same method as the [EV worked example](/blog/ev-worked-example): statement, overlap, leftover, then the industries underneath if you still want to lean in.

## Shared ground

Most people can share more than the argument admits:

- People look for ways to save, speculate, or hedge when cash and familiar assets feel uncertain.
- A scarce, transferable digital asset can be interesting even if you never want to “be a Bitcoin person”.
- What you should hold depends on your horizon, your sleep-at-night test, and whether you can afford a total loss.

That last line is also a floor: **this is not financial advice.**

The first-run overlap sits around **58%**. The invention can be real and the “you should” can still be too strong.

## The contested leftover

Three sentences carry the heat:

- Bitcoin is a **sound core holding** for a typical retail investor today.
- Bitcoin will become **everyday money** for ordinary payments.
- The usual objections — energy, regulation, lost keys, crime — are **overstated relative to the upside**.

A 1–2% satellite is not the same sentence as “you should invest.” Everyday payments are not the same as “can it move value.” Bundling every risk into “the usual FUD” is how a useful list becomes a loyalty test.

The demo leans treat the blanket core-holding claim and the everyday-money claim as too strong, and leave the objections question open. You can disagree. The point is to stake the sentence, not the tribe.

## Avenues if you want to double down

If you still want to lean in — for or against — read the machinery, not a ticker tape:

- **Custody and market structure.** Listed products, self-custody, and what “lost keys” actually means.
- **Payments rails.** Faster networks and merchant experiments versus the unit-of-account job.
- **Energy and hardware.** Mining, e-waste, and when those critiques bite.
- **Rules.** How quickly regulation can reprice the story.

Educational themes. Not a recommendation to buy or sell Bitcoin, or anything else.

## Later, not now

Play-money on this site is a practice round. **Paper trading** those themes, and any **FCA-regulated broker** handoff, come later. There are no live broker links and no tips.

Map the leftover first. If the gap is smaller than it looked, you can stop there. That still counts as a result.
